============================================================
UBUNTU LAPTOP: BRIGHTNESS FLICKERING TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
============================================================

COMMAND TO CHECK DRIVER:
lspci -k | grep -A3 VGA

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: DISABLE AUTOMATIC BRIGHTNESS
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Settings -> Power.
2. Toggle "Automatic Brightness" to OFF.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: SWITCH DISPLAY SERVER (WAYLAND VS X11)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log out of Ubuntu.
2. Click your username.
3. Click the GEAR ICON in the bottom-right corner.
4. Select "Ubuntu on Xorg" (X11) instead of "Ubuntu" (Wayland).
5. Log back in.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: DISABLE PANEL SELF REFRESH (INTEL/AMD INT. GRAPHICS)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open terminal and run:
   sudo nano /etc/default/grub

2. Find the line: GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX_DEFAULT="quiet splash"

3. Modify it based on your processor:
   - FOR INTEL: GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX_DEFAULT="quiet splash i915.enable_psr=0"
   - FOR AMD:   GRUB_CMDLINE_LINUX_DEFAULT="quiet splash amdgpu.dcfeaturemask=0"

4. Save file: Press Ctrl+O, then Enter. Exit: Press Ctrl+X.
5. Apply changes to system:
   sudo update-grub
6. Reboot your laptop.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: SWITCH TO PROPRIETARY NVIDIA DRIVERS (IF APPLICABLE)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Check available drivers:
   ubuntu-drivers devices
2. Automatically install the official recommended driver:
   sudo ubuntu-drivers autoinstall
3. Reboot your laptop.
